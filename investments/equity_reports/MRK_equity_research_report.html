Business Description

Merck is a global pharmaceutical company with a portfolio anchored by its oncology blockbuster Keytruda (pembrolizumab), alongside vaccines (including Gardasil), animal health products, and a broader specialty pharmaceutical pipeline. Keytruda represents a substantial portion of Merck's total revenue, making the company's long-term outlook heavily dependent on successful pipeline diversification ahead of Keytruda's eventual patent expiration.

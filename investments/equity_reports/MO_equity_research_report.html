Business Description

Altria Group, Inc., headquartered in Richmond, Virginia, is a holding company that manufactures and sells smokeable and oral tobacco products in the United States through its subsidiaries. Its smokeable products segment includes Philip Morris USA (PM USA, manufacturer of the Marlboro brand) and John Middleton Co. (Black & Mild large cigars and pipe tobacco). Its oral tobacco products segment includes U.S. Smokeless Tobacco Company (USSTC, moist smokeless tobacco under Copenhagen, Skoal, Red Seal, and Husky), Helix Innovations (oral nicotine pouches under the on! brand), and NJOY, LLC (e-vapor products under the NJOY ACE brand, though its market status remains an ongoing, disclosed guidance caveat). Altria was formerly the parent of Philip Morris International prior to PM's 2008 spin-off as a separately-traded international entity; Altria retained the U.S. domestic tobacco business exclusively, while PM operates internationally (see separate PM report in this series for the related but distinct international smoke-free growth story).

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

Altria operates exclusively in the U.S. tobacco and nicotine-products market, a structurally mature and declining category for traditional combustible cigarettes, offset partially by growth in oral nicotine pouches and (potentially, pending regulatory resolution) e-vapor products. Unlike Philip Morris International's international growth-oriented smoke-free transition (see separate PM report), Altria's domestic-only footprint means its growth algorithm relies more heavily on pricing power within a shrinking volume base, supplemented by newer oral nicotine products, rather than geographic expansion into new smoke-free markets.

Business Description

Chevron Corporation is one of the world's largest integrated energy companies, operating across upstream exploration and production, downstream refining and marketing, and increasingly power generation for large industrial and technology customers. The company's 2025 acquisition of Hess Corporation added significant offshore Guyana exposure and other high-quality assets to Chevron's portfolio, and integration synergies have run well ahead of initial targets. Chevron also maintains substantial international operations, including in Kazakhstan (the TCO field) and other major producing regions, alongside a growing U.S. shale (Permian) presence. Under CEO Mike Wirth, the company has emphasized capital discipline, balance-sheet strengthening, and new growth avenues such as dedicated power generation partnerships with technology companies (e.g., the Microsoft Project Kilby agreement).

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Record U.S. and international production growth, including Kazakhstan TCO field debottlenecking (260,000 to 320,000 barrels/day capacity increase)
  • Hess acquisition synergies running 50% ahead of target and six months ahead of schedule, expanding the Guyana asset contribution
  • New growth vector: a 20-year, 2.67-gigawatt power purchase agreement with Microsoft (Project Kilby), diversifying Chevron's revenue into dedicated power generation for technology/data-center customers

Key Expense Drivers

  • Capital expenditure associated with Hess integration and Kazakhstan TCO debottlenecking, guided to the lower end of the $18–19 billion FY2026 range
  • Ongoing cost-reduction program execution (already exceeded its $3 billion target six months early)

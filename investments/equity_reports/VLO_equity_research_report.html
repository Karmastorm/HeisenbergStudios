Business Description

Valero Energy is one of the world's largest independent petroleum refiners, operating a network of refineries primarily in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom, alongside ethanol production and renewable diesel operations. As a "pure-play" independent refiner (without significant integrated upstream production), Valero's earnings are especially directly leveraged to refining crack spreads relative to more diversified integrated peers.

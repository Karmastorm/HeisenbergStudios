Business Description

Exxon Mobil Corporation is one of the world's largest publicly traded integrated oil and gas companies, operating across the full energy value chain: Upstream (oil and gas exploration and production, including the Permian Basin and the Guyana offshore development), Energy Products (refining and fuels marketing), Chemical Products (petrochemicals), and Specialty Products (lubricants, basestocks, and other specialty materials). The company's 2024 acquisition of Pioneer Natural Resources significantly expanded its Permian Basin footprint, positioning ExxonMobil as one of the lowest-cost, highest-scale producers in the basin. Under CEO Darren Woods, the company has emphasized structural cost reduction, disciplined capital allocation, and continued development of its Guyana offshore assets as key growth pillars alongside the Permian.

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

ExxonMobil competes in the global integrated oil and gas industry, most directly against Chevron (see separate CVX report in this series) and other international oil majors. The industry remains subject to global crude oil and natural gas price cycles, though ExxonMobil's structural cost reductions and low-cost Permian/Guyana growth assets have supported earnings resilience relative to smaller, less diversified peers.

Business Description

Deere & Company manufactures and distributes agricultural, construction, forestry, and turf-care equipment worldwide, organized around Production and Precision Agriculture, Small Agriculture and Turf, Construction and Forestry, and a Financial Services segment that provides equipment financing to dealers and customers. The company has increasingly emphasized precision agriculture technology (autonomous tractors, See & Spray targeted-application systems, and connected-equipment data analytics) as a differentiated, higher-margin growth vector layered on top of its traditional heavy-equipment manufacturing business.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Large agricultural equipment (tractors, combines) unit sales, highly cyclical and tied to farm income, crop prices, and farmer capital-spending sentiment
  • Construction and forestry equipment sales, tied to infrastructure spending and non-residential construction activity
  • Precision agriculture technology and subscription revenue, an increasingly important higher-margin growth vector

Key Expense Drivers

  • Raw material and component costs (steel, engines, hydraulics), subject to commodity price and tariff volatility
  • R&D investment in autonomous and precision agriculture technology to maintain competitive differentiation

Operational The agricultural equipment cycle could remain depressed for longer than currently expected if crop prices and farm income fail to recover, extending the current earnings downturn.
Financial Currently rich trailing valuation multiples (38.1x P/E) are contingent on an eventual earnings recovery; a delayed or weaker-than-expected recovery could pressure the stock given it already trades near its 52-week high despite the earnings downturn.

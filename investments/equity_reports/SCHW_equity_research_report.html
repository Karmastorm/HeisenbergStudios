Business Description

Charles Schwab is a leading brokerage and banking company providing wealth management, securities brokerage, banking, asset management, and financial advisory services to individual and institutional clients, having significantly expanded its scale following the 2020 acquisition and subsequent integration of TD Ameritrade.

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

Charles Schwab competes in the retail brokerage, wealth management, and banking industry against Fidelity, Morgan Stanley's E*TRADE (see separate MS report in this series), Robinhood, and other discount and full-service brokerages, having significantly expanded its scale and market share through the TD Ameritrade acquisition.

Competitive Advantage ("Economic Moat")

Charles Schwab's moat rests on its massively expanded scale following the TD Ameritrade acquisition, its integrated brokerage-and-banking model that captures net interest income on client cash, and high switching costs for clients with consolidated brokerage, banking, and advisory relationships.

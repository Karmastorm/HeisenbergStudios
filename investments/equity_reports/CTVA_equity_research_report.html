Business Description

Corteva, Inc. develops and markets seed genetics (including corn, soybean, and other row-crop seed products) and crop protection chemicals (herbicides, insecticides, fungicides) for farmers globally, having been spun off from the former DowDuPont in 2019. The company is now itself reportedly planning a further separation into two independent public companies, splitting its seed/genetics business (to be named "Vylor") from its crop protection business, a transaction expected to close around October 1, 2026.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Seed genetics sales, tied to farmer planting decisions, crop acreage, and technology/trait adoption rates for corn, soybean, and other row crops
  • Crop protection chemical sales, tied to pest, weed, and disease pressure and farmer input-spending levels
  • New product introductions and technology licensing, an important growth lever in both the seed and crop protection businesses

Key Expense Drivers

  • Research and development investment in seed genetics and crop protection active ingredients, a significant ongoing cost for maintaining competitive product pipelines
  • Raw material costs for crop protection chemical manufacturing, subject to petrochemical commodity price volatility
  • Costs associated with the pending business separation, a near-term one-time expense category

Business Description

Northrop Grumman Corporation is a global aerospace and defense technology company operating through four principal segments: Aeronautics Systems (manned and unmanned aircraft, including the B-21 Raider stealth bomber), Space Systems (satellites, space launch, and missile defense), Defense Systems (battle management and weapons systems), and Mission Systems (sensors, radar, and cyber capabilities). The company derives the substantial majority of its revenue from the U.S. government, primarily the Department of Defense and intelligence community, under multi-year, often cost-plus and fixed-price development and production contracts.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • U.S. defense budget appropriations and multi-year program funding, particularly for the B-21 Raider bomber and Sentinel ICBM modernization programs
  • Space systems contracts, including missile defense, satellite, and space launch programs for the U.S. government and allied nations
  • International defense sales through foreign military sales channels, subject to U.S. export control approval

Key Expense Drivers

  • Program development and production costs, including materials, subcontractor costs, and skilled engineering labor
  • Fixed-price development program cost overruns, a periodic risk on next-generation platforms such as the B-21

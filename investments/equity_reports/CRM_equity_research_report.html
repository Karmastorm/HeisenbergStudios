Business Description

Salesforce, Inc. provides cloud-based enterprise software for customer relationship management, organized around Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud, Commerce Cloud, and the Salesforce Platform, alongside acquired businesses including Slack (workplace collaboration), Tableau (analytics/visualization), and MuleSoft (application integration). The company has recently emphasized Data Cloud (a unified customer data platform) and Agentforce (autonomous AI agents that can execute customer service, sales, and other business workflows) as key growth vectors layered on top of its core CRM subscription base.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Core Sales Cloud and Service Cloud subscription renewal and seat/usage expansion within the existing enterprise customer base
  • Data Cloud and Agentforce adoption and monetization as AI-driven upsell opportunities across the installed base
  • Cross-sell of Slack, Tableau, and MuleSoft into the broader Salesforce customer relationships

Key Expense Drivers

  • Sales and marketing expense, historically a very large cost line for Salesforce's direct-sales-driven go-to-market model
  • R&D investment in AI/agentic product development (Agentforce) and cloud infrastructure costs to support usage-based AI workloads

Business Description

Booking Holdings Inc. is the world's largest online travel company by gross bookings, operating a portfolio of leading travel brands including Booking.com (accommodation reservations), Priceline, Agoda, Kayak (travel search/metasearch), and OpenTable (restaurant reservations). The company connects travelers with hotels, alternative accommodations, rental cars, flights, and experiences globally, monetizing primarily through commissions on completed bookings. Under longtime CEO Glenn Fogel, Booking has pursued a "Connected Trip" strategy aiming to integrate flights, accommodations, ground transportation, and experiences into a single seamless booking journey, while investing in AI-driven customer service and personalization tools to improve unit economics and conversion.

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

Booking Holdings competes in the global online travel industry, most directly against Expedia Group, with Airbnb competing in alternative accommodations and Trip.com Group in Asia-focused markets. The Middle East conflict's impact on long-haul flight demand in Q2 2026 illustrates the industry's ongoing sensitivity to geopolitical shocks, even as underlying accommodation-booking demand has remained resilient.

Booking Holdings' moat derives from Booking.com's unmatched global accommodation supply density (particularly in Europe and Asia-Pacific independent/boutique properties), powerful brand recognition across its portfolio, and a data/scale advantage in performance marketing that smaller competitors cannot match. Its early and methodical AI investment supports the durability of this position as search and discovery behavior evolves.

Business Description

Lockheed Martin Corporation is a global aerospace, defense, and security company organized around four segments: Aeronautics (F-35 Joint Strike Fighter, F-16, and other fixed-wing military aircraft), Missiles and Fire Control (precision-strike missiles and air/missile defense systems), Rotary and Mission Systems (helicopters and naval/combat systems), and Space (satellites, missile defense, and space exploration systems). The company derives the substantial majority of its revenue from the U.S. government, primarily the Department of Defense, alongside international allied-nation defense sales.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • F-35 Joint Strike Fighter program production and sustainment revenue, the company's single largest program
  • Missiles and Fire Control demand, benefiting from elevated global munitions replenishment amid heightened geopolitical tensions
  • International defense sales to allied nations, an increasingly important growth vector amid rising global defense budgets

Key Expense Drivers

  • Program cost overruns on fixed-price and cost-plus defense contracts, a persistent industry risk factor that has historically affected margin performance
  • R&D investment in next-generation defense technology (hypersonics, directed energy, and space systems) to maintain competitive position against rivals

Business Description

Colgate-Palmolive Company, headquartered in New York, is a global consumer products company serving people in over 200 countries and territories, organized around four core categories: Oral Care (Colgate toothpaste and oral hygiene products, a globally dominant category leader), Personal Care, Home Care, and Pet Nutrition (Hill's Science Diet and Hill's Prescription Diet). The company's Hill's Pet Nutrition business has been a particular recent growth and margin-improvement focus, including a strategic decision to exit lower-margin private-label pet food production in favor of its own branded portfolio — a deliberate mix-shift that creates a near-term organic-sales-growth headwind (0.4 points in Q2 2026) in exchange for improved long-term profitability and brand focus. Colgate-Palmolive has significant, often-underappreciated emerging-market exposure (India, Brazil, Mexico, China among its strongest recent growth markets), which frequently outpaces its North American business.

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

Colgate-Palmolive operates in the global household and personal products industry, competing most directly against Procter & Gamble (see separate PG report in this series) and Kimberly-Clark (see separate KMB report) in personal/home care, and against Mars (Pedigree/Royal Canin) and Nestlé Purina in pet nutrition via Hill's. The company's oral care category leadership (Colgate is a globally dominant toothpaste brand) provides a distinct competitive anchor less directly overlapping with P&G's broader portfolio breadth.

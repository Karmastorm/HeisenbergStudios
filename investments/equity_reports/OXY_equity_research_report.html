Business Description

Occidental Petroleum is a diversified energy and chemicals company operating three primary business segments: Oil & Gas (upstream exploration and production, with a significant Permian Basin position following the 2023 acquisition of CrownRock), OxyChem (basic chemicals manufacturing), and Low Carbon Ventures (including direct air capture and carbon management initiatives). The company holds a notable long-term strategic equity investment from Berkshire Hathaway.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Upstream oil and gas production, heavily weighted toward the Permian Basin following the 2023 CrownRock acquisition
  • OxyChem basic chemicals segment, providing some earnings diversification away from pure crude-oil price exposure
  • Emerging Low Carbon Ventures business, including direct air capture (DAC) technology and carbon management services

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

Occidental competes in the U.S. oil and gas exploration and production industry, most directly against other large-cap E&P and integrated producers such as ConocoPhillips and EOG Resources (see separate COP and EOG reports in this series). Occidental's diversification into chemicals (OxyChem) and low-carbon ventures (direct air capture) differentiates it somewhat from pure-play E&P peers, though its earnings remain heavily leveraged to crude oil price movements.

Occidental's moat rests on its expanded Permian Basin scale following the CrownRock acquisition, its unique OxyChem chemicals diversification, its early-mover position in direct air capture and carbon management technology, and its long-term strategic relationship with Berkshire Hathaway as a major shareholder.

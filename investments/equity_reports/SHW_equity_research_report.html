Business Description

The Sherwin-Williams Company develops, manufactures, distributes, and sells paint, coatings, and related products through three segments: The Americas Group (a network of company-operated paint stores serving professional painting contractors), Consumer Brands Group (retail paint and coatings sold through home-improvement and hardware retailers), and Performance Coatings Group (industrial coatings for automotive refinish, protective, marine, and packaging end markets). The company's direct-to-professional paint-store network is a key competitive differentiator, providing contractors with convenient access, tinting services, and credit terms not typically available through big-box retail channels.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Professional painting contractor demand for residential and commercial repaint and new-construction projects, sold through the company's paint-store network
  • Retail consumer paint sales through home-improvement and hardware retail channels
  • Industrial coatings sales tied to automotive refinish, protective, and packaging end markets

Key Expense Drivers

  • Raw material costs, particularly titanium dioxide and petroleum-based resins, subject to commodity price volatility
  • Paint-store network operating costs, including real estate leases and store-level labor

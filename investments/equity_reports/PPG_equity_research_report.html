Business Description

PPG Industries, Inc. manufactures and sells paints, coatings, and specialty materials globally, serving architectural (residential and commercial building), automotive OEM and refinish, industrial (general industrial, packaging), and protective and marine coatings end markets. The company operates through Performance Coatings and Industrial Coatings segments, distributing products both through its own retail store network and third-party retail and distribution channels.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Architectural coatings demand tied to residential and commercial construction and repaint activity
  • Automotive OEM and refinish coatings sales tied to global vehicle production and collision-repair volumes
  • Industrial and protective/marine coatings sales tied to broader manufacturing and infrastructure activity

Key Expense Drivers

  • Raw material costs, particularly titanium dioxide and petroleum-based resins, subject to commodity price volatility
  • Ongoing cost-reduction and restructuring program expenses aimed at improving margins amid muted top-line growth

Deal Summary

On October 11, 2023, ExxonMobil announced a definitive agreement to acquire Pioneer Natural Resources in an all-stock transaction valued at approximately $59.5 billion (enterprise value, including net debt). Under the terms of the deal, each share of PXD common stock was exchanged for 2.3234 shares of ExxonMobil common stock at closing. The transaction — one of the largest in the U.S. oil and gas sector in recent years — significantly expanded ExxonMobil's footprint in the Permian Basin, combining Pioneer's roughly 850,000 net acres in the Midland Basin with ExxonMobil's existing Permian position to create the largest unconventional resource holder in the basin. The deal closed on May 3, 2024, following regulatory clearance from the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (which required, as a condition, that Pioneer's outgoing CEO not join ExxonMobil's board, addressing antitrust concerns related to alleged coordination with OPEC on production levels). Upon closing, PXD shares were converted into XOM shares and PXD ceased to exist as an independent public company.

Business Description

S&P Global is a leading provider of credit ratings, benchmarks, analytics, and workflow solutions to the global capital and commodity markets, operating across Ratings, Market Intelligence, Commodity Insights, Mobility, and Indices segments. Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

S&P Global competes in the global financial data, ratings, and index industry against Moody's in credit ratings, and various data/analytics providers in market intelligence, alongside index competitors in the benchmarking business. Its Ratings and Indices franchises in particular benefit from durable oligopolistic industry structures.

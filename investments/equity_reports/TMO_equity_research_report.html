Business Description

Thermo Fisher Scientific is a leading global life sciences tools and services company operating across four segments: Life Sciences Solutions, Analytical Instruments, Specialty Diagnostics, and Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services, serving pharmaceutical, biotechnology, academic, government, and clinical customers worldwide.

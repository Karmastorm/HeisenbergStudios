Business Description

Welltower Inc. is a real estate investment trust that owns and operates senior housing communities, outpatient medical facilities, and other healthcare-related real estate across the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada, partnering with regional and national operators to manage day-to-day facility operations while Welltower focuses on capital allocation and portfolio strategy. The company benefits from favorable long-term demographic tailwinds as the aging population drives increasing demand for senior housing and healthcare real estate.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Senior housing occupancy rates and rental rate growth, benefiting from favorable demographic tailwinds as the senior population expands
  • Same-store net operating income growth across the existing portfolio, alongside continued portfolio expansion through acquisitions
  • Outpatient medical facility leasing revenue, providing a more stable, less cyclical complement to the senior housing portfolio

Key Expense Drivers

  • Property operating expenses passed through from third-party operators managing day-to-day senior housing community operations, including labor and care-related costs
  • Depreciation on the substantially expanded real estate portfolio following a period of significant acquisition activity, a key driver of the gap between GAAP net income and cash-generating FFO

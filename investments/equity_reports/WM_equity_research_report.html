Business Description

Waste Management, Inc. provides collection, transfer, disposal, recycling, and resource-recovery services to residential, commercial, industrial, and municipal customers across North America, operating an extensive network of landfills, transfer stations, and recycling facilities. Following its 2024 acquisition of Stericycle, the company has expanded into regulated medical waste collection and secure information destruction, diversifying its revenue base and adding a higher-margin, less commoditized service line alongside its traditional solid-waste operations.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Collection and disposal pricing and volume across residential, commercial, and industrial customer channels, generally recurring and contractually recurring in nature
  • Landfill gas-to-energy and renewable natural gas facility investments, an increasingly important higher-margin growth vector
  • Regulated medical waste and secure information destruction services acquired via Stericycle, diversifying the revenue base

Key Expense Drivers

  • Labor, fuel, and vehicle maintenance costs across the collection fleet, along with landfill operating and post-closure environmental costs
  • Integration and transition costs associated with the Stericycle acquisition, a near-term drag on reported net income

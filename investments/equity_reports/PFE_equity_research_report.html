Business Description

Pfizer is a global biopharmaceutical company operating across Biopharma, PC1, and Pfizer Ignite segments, with a diversified portfolio spanning cardiovascular disease (Eliquis), vaccines (Prevnar family, Comirnaty COVID-19 vaccine), oncology, and other specialty therapeutic areas, following a multi-year period of post-pandemic revenue normalization from its COVID-19 product peak.

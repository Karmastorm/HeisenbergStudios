Business Description

Newmont Corporation is the world's largest gold mining company by production volume, operating a globally diversified portfolio of mines across North America, South America, Australia, and Africa following its 2023 acquisition of Newcrest Mining. The company also produces meaningful quantities of copper, silver, zinc, and lead as by-products, and has pursued a portfolio-optimization strategy involving the divestiture of certain non-core assets to focus capital on its highest-return "Tier 1" mining operations.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Gold price, which has historically served as a safe-haven asset during periods of macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainty, driving the recent strong price rally reflected in Newmont's results
  • Gold production volumes across the company's globally diversified mine portfolio
  • Copper and other by-product metal sales, an increasingly meaningful secondary revenue stream following the Newcrest acquisition

Key Expense Drivers

  • Mining and processing costs, including energy, labor, and consumable inputs, which vary by mine site and ore grade
  • Capital expenditure on mine-life extension projects and continued portfolio optimization/integration costs following the Newcrest acquisition

Competitive RivalryModerate — competition with Barrick Mining, Agnico Eagle Mines, and other major global gold producers, with Newmont differentiated by its scale and Tier 1 asset portfolio following the Newcrest acquisition

Competitive Advantage ("Economic Moat")

Newmont's moat is rooted in its position as the world's largest gold producer by scale, a globally diversified portfolio of long-life Tier 1 mining assets that reduces single-mine operational risk, and significant operating leverage to gold price increases given the company's substantial fixed-cost production base.

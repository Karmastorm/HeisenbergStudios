Business Description

Public Service Enterprise Group Incorporated is a New Jersey-based diversified energy holding company operating primarily through Public Service Electric and Gas Company (PSE&G), a regulated electric and gas transmission and distribution utility serving approximately 2.4 million electric and 1.9 million gas customers in New Jersey, and PSEG Power, which operates a nuclear generation fleet and other competitive generation assets. The company has increasingly focused its growth strategy on PSE&G's regulated rate base and the stable, carbon-free output of its nuclear fleet.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Regulated electric and gas transmission and distribution revenue from PSE&G, driven by customer growth, rate base expansion, and approved rate case outcomes.
  • Nuclear generation output from PSEG Power, benefiting from PJM capacity market pricing and power purchase agreements.
  • Growing electricity demand in New Jersey, including data-center-related load growth in the broader PJM market.
  • Grid modernization and reliability investment supporting rate-base growth.

Key Expense Drivers

  • Capital expenditure on grid modernization, transmission infrastructure, and nuclear plant maintenance and life extension.
  • Fuel and nuclear operating costs for the PSEG Power generation fleet.
  • Interest expense on debt used to fund the company's capital investment program.
  • Operations and maintenance costs across the regulated utility and nuclear generation businesses.

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

PEG operates within the PJM Interconnection footprint, a region increasingly characterized by surging electricity demand from data centers and industrial reshoring, layered on historically slow load growth. PSEG's regulated transmission and distribution business benefits from stable, rate-regulated returns, while its nuclear generation fleet provides carbon-free, dispatchable capacity increasingly valued by data-center and hyperscale customers seeking reliable, low-carbon power.

Investment Risks

Operational

Nuclear plant operational and refueling outage risk at PSEG Power, alongside severe weather exposure typical of the Mid-Atlantic region for the PSE&G distribution network.

Financial

Ongoing capital expenditure requirements for grid modernization and nuclear plant maintenance create sensitivity to interest rates and capital market access.

Regulatory

Rate case outcomes with the New Jersey Board of Public Utilities directly determine allowed returns on invested capital, and nuclear plant relicensing and PJM capacity market rule changes could affect PSEG Power's economics.

Legal

The company faces ongoing legal and regulatory risk related to nuclear plant safety and decommissioning obligations, as well as environmental and infrastructure siting litigation.

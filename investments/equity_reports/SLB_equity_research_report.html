Business Description

SLB (formerly Schlumberger Limited) is the world's largest oilfield services company, providing technology, project management, and information solutions to oil and gas exploration and production companies globally. The company's business spans reservoir characterization, drilling, production, and digital/data solutions, and its recent acquisition of ChampionX added production chemicals and artificial lift capabilities that broaden SLB's exposure across the well lifecycle. Under CEO Olivier Le Peuch, SLB has increasingly emphasized digital and AI-enabled offerings, including a newly announced push into data-center-related solutions, positioning the company at the intersection of energy infrastructure and the broader technology/AI infrastructure buildout.

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

SLB competes in the global oilfield services and equipment industry, a segment distinct from the exploration & production and integrated-major peers elsewhere in this report series (XOM, CVX, COP, EOG). As an oilfield-services provider, SLB's revenue is driven by E&P companies' capital spending activity, which management characterizes as showing early upcycle characteristics as global oil and gas producers begin replenishing inventory and diversifying supply.

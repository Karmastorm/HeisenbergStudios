Business Description

Danaher is a global science and technology company operating across life sciences and diagnostics, following a multi-year portfolio transformation that included the 2023 spinoff of its environmental and applied solutions business (now Veralto). The company's remaining portfolio focuses on bioprocessing, genomics, and diagnostics instrumentation and consumables.

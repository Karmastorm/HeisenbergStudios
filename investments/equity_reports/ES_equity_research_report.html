Business Description

Eversource Energy is a public utility holding company providing regulated electric transmission and distribution, natural gas delivery, and water utility services across Connecticut, Massachusetts, and New Hampshire through subsidiaries including The Connecticut Light and Power Company, NSTAR Electric, and Eversource Gas Company. The company has in recent years worked to simplify its business mix and reduce exposure to non-regulated, higher-risk ventures such as offshore wind development, refocusing on its core regulated transmission and distribution operations.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Regulated electric transmission and distribution revenue across Connecticut, Massachusetts, and New Hampshire, driven by customer growth, rate base expansion, and approved rate case outcomes.
  • Regulated natural gas distribution revenue.
  • Regulated water utility revenue in Massachusetts.
  • Grid modernization and reliability investment supporting rate-base growth.

Key Expense Drivers

  • Capital expenditure on grid modernization, storm resiliency, and regulated infrastructure across three New England states.
  • Operations and maintenance costs across a multi-state electric, gas, and water utility operation.
  • Interest expense on debt used to fund the company's capital investment program.
  • Any residual costs or charges associated with the company's prior offshore wind divestiture.

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

The U.S. regulated electric, gas, and water utility industry is characterized by stable, rate-regulated returns on capital investment. Eversource's New England service territory faces some of the nation's highest electricity costs and most stringent clean energy mandates, creating both substantial approved capital investment opportunity and elevated regulatory and political scrutiny over rate increases.

Competitive Advantage

Eversource's scale across Connecticut, Massachusetts, and New Hampshire, combined with its recent strategic simplification away from higher-risk offshore wind development, provides a more focused, lower-risk regulated utility earnings base than in prior years, though the company's high-cost New England service territory carries elevated political and regulatory scrutiny.

This ratio is consistent with the recurring data-aggregation error pattern observed across multiple tickers in this series. We have excluded these TTM figures from the summary table above and used FY2024/FY2025 annual figures as the analytical base. Separately, and distinct from the TTM aggregation issue, the source tool itself flagged that the TTM net income aggregate includes disclosed negative quarterly net income in Q3 2024 (-$549 million) and Q4 2023 (-$442 million) — real, disclosed loss quarters rather than data errors, but ones that materially depress any trailing-period comparison and likely explain much of FY2024's unusually low net income base versus FY2025's more-than-doubling. Readers should independently verify the specific cause of these loss quarters (potentially related to prior offshore wind divestiture charges) via ES's most recent 10-Q and 10-K filings.

Investment Risks

Operational

Exposure to severe New England weather events (nor'easters, ice storms) that can damage infrastructure and require substantial restoration capital expenditure across its electric, gas, and water utility operations.

Financial

The recent history of significant negative-net-income quarters warrants monitoring for recurrence; ongoing capital expenditure requirements create sensitivity to interest rates and capital market access.

Regulatory

Rate case outcomes with Connecticut, Massachusetts, and New Hampshire regulators directly determine allowed returns on invested capital, amid some of the nation's highest regional electricity costs and most stringent clean energy mandates.

Legal

The company faces ongoing legal and regulatory risk related to any residual matters from its prior offshore wind investments and divestiture, as well as general infrastructure and environmental compliance litigation.

Business Description

Prologis, Inc. is a real estate investment trust that owns, operates, and develops logistics and distribution facilities across the Americas, Europe, and Asia, leasing warehouse and distribution space to a diversified tenant base including e-commerce companies, third-party logistics providers, and traditional manufacturers and retailers. The company has increasingly emphasized value-added services, including data center and energy solutions built on its logistics real estate platform, and has announced a major acquisition (Segro) to expand its European scale.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Rental rate growth and occupancy levels across the company's global logistics real estate portfolio, driven by e-commerce and supply-chain reconfiguration demand
  • Development and leasing of new logistics facilities in strategically located, high-barrier-to-entry markets near population centers and transportation infrastructure
  • Growing contribution from value-added services, including energy solutions (solar, battery storage) deployed across the property portfolio

Key Expense Drivers

  • Property operating expenses, including maintenance, insurance, and property taxes across a large global real estate portfolio
  • Interest expense on debt used to fund acquisitions and development, including financing associated with the pending Segro transaction

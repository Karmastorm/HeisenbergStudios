Business Description

Abbott Laboratories is a diversified global health care company operating across four segments: Established Pharmaceuticals, Diagnostics, Nutrition, and Medical Devices (including its FreeStyle Libre continuous glucose monitoring franchise), serving markets across both developed and emerging economies.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Medical Devices segment revenue, led by the FreeStyle Libre continuous glucose monitoring franchise, a key growth driver
  • Diagnostics segment revenue, including both core laboratory and rapid/point-of-care diagnostic testing
  • Nutrition and Established Pharmaceuticals segment revenue, providing diversified, more stable cash flow streams, particularly in emerging markets

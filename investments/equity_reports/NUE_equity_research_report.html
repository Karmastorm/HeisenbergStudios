Business Description

Nucor Corporation is the largest steel producer in the United States, manufacturing steel products through an electric-arc-furnace (mini-mill) production model that primarily uses recycled scrap steel as feedstock, offering greater operational and cost flexibility than traditional integrated blast-furnace steelmaking. The company operates across steel mills, steel products (including joist, deck, and fabricated construction products), and raw materials segments, serving construction, automotive, energy, and industrial end markets.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Steel pricing, which is cyclical and sensitive to domestic construction activity, global steel trade dynamics, and tariff policy
  • Steel shipment volumes across the company's mills and downstream steel products businesses
  • Non-residential and infrastructure construction demand, a key driver for the steel products (joist, deck, fabricated construction products) segment

Key Expense Drivers

  • Scrap steel feedstock costs, the primary raw material input for Nucor's electric-arc-furnace production model
  • Energy costs (electricity) required for electric-arc-furnace steel production

Competitive Advantage ("Economic Moat")

Nucor's moat is rooted in its electric-arc-furnace production model, which provides greater cost flexibility and faster response to demand fluctuations than traditional integrated blast-furnace competitors, combined with scale, geographic manufacturing diversification across the U.S., and a diversified downstream steel products business that provides some earnings stability versus pure commodity steel producers.

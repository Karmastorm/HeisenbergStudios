Business Description

Air Products and Chemicals, Inc. is a global industrial gases company supplying atmospheric gases (oxygen, nitrogen, argon), process and specialty gases (hydrogen, helium, syngas), and related equipment to customers in refining, chemicals, metals, electronics, and manufacturing end markets. The company has in recent years pursued a large capital program in clean/"blue" and "green" hydrogen production and gasification megaprojects tied to the energy transition, though it announced in June 2026 that it would exit several of these major projects, resulting in a substantial impairment and restructuring charge.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Long-term on-site gas supply contracts with industrial customers, typically including take-or-pay volume commitments providing recurring revenue
  • Merchant gas sales tied to broader industrial production trends
  • Clean hydrogen and gasification project revenue, a strategic growth area now being scaled back following the June 2026 project-exit announcement

Key Expense Drivers

  • Energy costs required for air separation and gas production, substantially passed through via contract pricing mechanisms
  • Capital expenditure on new production facilities, though this is expected to moderate following the exit from several megaprojects
  • The FY2025 project-exit impairment and restructuring charge (approximately $2.9B pre-tax), a significant one-time expense

The TTM net income figure of approximately -$47.3M (versus the FY2025 full-year loss of -$394.5M) suggests sequential improvement in quarters following the initial impairment recognition, consistent with the charge being a discrete, largely non-recurring event rather than an ongoing operational deterioration.

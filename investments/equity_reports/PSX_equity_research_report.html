Business Description

Phillips 66 is an integrated downstream energy provider operating across four core segments: refining, midstream (including its consolidated interest in midstream logistics assets), chemicals (through the CPChem joint venture), and renewable fuels. Its refining network converts crude oil into gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, and other refined products, with earnings closely tied to regional crack spreads — the margin between crude input costs and refined product prices — which are highly sensitive to global supply disruptions, seasonal demand, and refinery utilization rates across the industry.

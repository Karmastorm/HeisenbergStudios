Business Description

Amgen Inc. is a global biotechnology company that discovers, develops, manufactures, and markets human therapeutics, with a portfolio spanning inflammation, bone health, cardiovascular disease, oncology, and rare disease. The company significantly expanded its rare-disease and inflammation footprint through its 2023 acquisition of Horizon Therapeutics (adding Tepezza, Uplizna, and Krystexxa), while continuing to manage biosimilar competition against long-standing legacy products such as Enbrel and Otezla and investing in a pipeline of newer launches including TEZSPIRE, IMDELLTRA, and the obesity candidate MariTide.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Volume and pricing trends across the core inflammation, bone health, and cardiovascular franchises (Prolia, Repatha, Evenity), balanced against biosimilar erosion on older products such as Enbrel and Otezla
  • Continued ramp of the Horizon Therapeutics rare-disease portfolio (Tepezza, Uplizna, Krystexxa) acquired in 2023
  • Uptake of newer product launches, including TEZSPIRE (severe asthma) and IMDELLTRA (oncology), and clinical/regulatory progress on pipeline candidates such as MariTide

Key Expense Drivers

  • Research and development spending across a broad late-stage and early-stage pipeline, including the obesity/incretin program
  • Interest expense associated with debt taken on to fund the Horizon Therapeutics acquisition, alongside ongoing manufacturing and commercialization costs for the expanded product portfolio

Business Description

JPMorgan Chase & Co. is the largest bank holding company in the United States by assets, operating across four primary segments: Consumer & Community Banking, Corporate & Investment Bank, Commercial Banking, and Asset & Wealth Management. This diversification provides earnings resilience across varying interest rate and credit cycle environments relative to more narrowly focused financial institutions.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Net interest income from its large consumer and commercial deposit and lending base
  • Investment banking fees and trading revenue from its leading Corporate & Investment Bank franchise
  • Asset & Wealth Management fee income, benefiting from scale and strong long-term investment performance

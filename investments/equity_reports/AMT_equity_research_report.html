Business Description

American Tower Corporation is a global real estate investment trust that owns, operates, and develops multitenant communications real estate, primarily wireless communications towers, leasing space on its towers to wireless carriers and other tenants under long-term contracts that typically include built-in rent escalators. The company has also expanded into data centers through its CoreSite acquisition, diversifying its communications infrastructure platform beyond traditional tower leasing.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Tower tenancy growth and contractual rent escalators embedded in long-term leases with wireless carrier tenants
  • New tower development and acquisitions, particularly in international markets with growing wireless network investment
  • Data center segment (CoreSite) revenue growth, driven by increasing enterprise and hyperscale cloud demand for interconnection and colocation services

Key Expense Drivers

  • Ground lease and property tax expenses associated with the company's large global tower portfolio
  • Interest expense on debt used to fund tower acquisitions, international expansion, and the CoreSite data center investment

Competitive Advantage ("Economic Moat")

American Tower's moat is rooted in its irreplaceable portfolio of well-located tower sites, long-term contracted leases with built-in rent escalators, and high tenant switching costs, providing highly durable and predictable cash flows across economic cycles.

Business Description

Digital Realty Trust, Inc. is a real estate investment trust that owns and operates a global platform of data centers, providing colocation, interconnection, and hyperscale data center solutions to enterprises, cloud service providers, and network operators. The company competes directly with Equinix in the global data center REIT space and has increasingly emphasized capital recycling through joint ventures and asset dispositions to fund its substantial hyperscale capacity expansion program while managing balance sheet leverage.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Data center leasing revenue growth driven by cabinet/space utilization and pricing, particularly for hyperscale and AI-workload-driven capacity
  • Interconnection revenue, a high-margin business benefiting from network-effect dynamics similar to peer data center REITs
  • Capacity expansion and new data center development to meet accelerating AI and hyperscale cloud infrastructure demand

Key Expense Drivers

  • Power costs, a significant and rising operating expense given the energy intensity of data center operations, particularly with AI workload growth
  • Capital expenditure on new data center development and capacity expansion, partially funded through joint-venture and capital-recycling transactions

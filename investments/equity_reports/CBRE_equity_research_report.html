Business Description

CBRE Group, Inc. is the world's largest commercial real estate services and investment firm, providing brokerage services (property leasing and sales), property and facilities management, valuation and appraisal, loan servicing, and investment management to owners, investors, and occupiers of commercial real estate globally. Unlike the REITs elsewhere in this sector, CBRE does not own significant real estate itself; instead, it earns fee-based revenue for services rendered, a structurally different, less capital-intensive, but also more transaction-volume-sensitive business model.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Commercial real estate transaction volume (leasing and capital markets/sales activity), which is cyclical and sensitive to interest rates and broader economic conditions
  • Growth in the company's more recurring-revenue facilities and property management and investment management segments, which provide a stabilizing counterbalance to transactional brokerage revenue
  • Global platform scale, allowing CBRE to serve multinational corporate occupiers and institutional real estate investors across markets

Key Expense Drivers

  • Commission and compensation expense for brokerage professionals, a variable cost that scales with transaction revenue
  • Technology and platform investment to support the company's global services and data/analytics capabilities

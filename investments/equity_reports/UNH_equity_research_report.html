Business Description

UnitedHealth Group is the largest health insurer in the United States, operating through UnitedHealthcare (health benefits) and Optum (health services, including OptumHealth, OptumInsight, and OptumRx pharmacy benefit management), providing an integrated model spanning insurance, care delivery, and pharmacy services.

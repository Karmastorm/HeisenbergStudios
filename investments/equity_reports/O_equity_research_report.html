Business Description

Realty Income Corporation is a real estate investment trust that owns a highly diversified portfolio of freestanding commercial properties across the United States and Europe, leasing them to retail, industrial, gaming, and other tenants under long-term net-lease agreements in which tenants are typically responsible for property taxes, insurance, and maintenance. The company is widely known for its monthly dividend payment history and has increasingly diversified beyond traditional retail into industrial, gaming, and other property types to reduce concentration risk.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Base rental income across a highly diversified net-lease property portfolio, with contractual rent escalators providing built-in growth
  • Continued acquisition activity, funded through equity and debt issuance, expanding the portfolio's scale and diversification
  • Portfolio diversification into industrial, gaming, and international (primarily European) properties, reducing traditional retail concentration risk

Key Expense Drivers

  • Interest expense on debt used to fund the company's substantial ongoing acquisition activity, sensitive to prevailing interest rate conditions
  • General and administrative costs associated with managing a large, geographically diversified property portfolio

Competitive Advantage ("Economic Moat")

Realty Income's moat is rooted in its scale and cost-of-capital advantages as one of the largest net-lease REITs, a highly diversified tenant and property-type base that reduces concentration risk, and a decades-long track record of consistent monthly dividend payments that has built a loyal, income-focused investor base.

Business Description

Public Storage is a real estate investment trust that owns, operates, and manages self-storage facilities, renting storage units to residential and commercial customers on flexible, typically month-to-month lease terms. The company has recently expanded its scale through the acquisition of National Storage Affiliates and Public Storage Canada, adding to its already-largest position in the fragmented U.S. self-storage industry, and maintains a minority equity stake in European self-storage operations.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Occupancy rates and achieved rental rates across the self-storage facility portfolio, which can be adjusted relatively quickly given short-term lease structures
  • Ancillary revenue from tenant insurance and merchandise sales at storage facilities
  • Continued portfolio expansion through acquisitions, including the recently completed National Storage Affiliates and Public Storage Canada transactions

Key Expense Drivers

  • Property operating expenses, including property tax, insurance, and facility maintenance costs across a large, geographically dispersed portfolio
  • Interest expense and integration costs associated with the recently completed National Storage Affiliates and Public Storage Canada acquisitions

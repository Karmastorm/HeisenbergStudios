Business Description

AbbVie is a global biopharmaceutical company with a portfolio spanning immunology (Skyrizi, Rinvoq, and the legacy Humira franchise), oncology, aesthetics (Botox and Juvederm via its Allergan acquisition), and neuroscience. The company has successfully transitioned its immunology franchise growth engine from Humira (facing biosimilar competition since 2023) to next-generation therapies Skyrizi and Rinvoq.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Skyrizi and Rinvoq immunology franchise growth, which has more than offset Humira's biosimilar-driven revenue decline
  • Aesthetics segment revenue from Botox Cosmetic and Juvederm, acquired via the 2020 Allergan acquisition
  • Oncology and neuroscience pipeline products contributing to diversified revenue growth

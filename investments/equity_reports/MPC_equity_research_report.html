Business Description

Marathon Petroleum Corporation is one of the largest petroleum refining, marketing, and midstream companies in the United States, operating a nationwide refining network alongside a retail marketing business and a controlling interest in MPLX, a publicly traded midstream logistics partnership. The company's earnings are heavily leveraged to refining crack spreads — the margin between crude oil input costs and refined product output prices — which fluctuate with regional supply/demand balances, seasonal driving-season demand, and refinery-capacity utilization across the industry. MPLX provides a more stable, fee-based midstream earnings stream that partially offsets the cyclicality of the core refining business.

Industry Overview & Competitive Positioning

Marathon Petroleum competes in the U.S. petroleum refining and marketing industry, most directly against Valero Energy and Phillips 66 (see separate VLO and PSX reports in this series). Refining is an inherently cyclical, commodity-margin-driven business; the entire sector benefited from historically elevated crack spreads in Q2 2026, meaning the earnings surge is likely to be industry-wide rather than solely reflective of MPC-specific execution.

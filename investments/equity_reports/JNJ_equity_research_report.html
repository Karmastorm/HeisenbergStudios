Business Description

Johnson & Johnson is a diversified global health care company operating across two primary segments following its 2023 Kenvue consumer health spinoff: Innovative Medicine (pharmaceuticals) and MedTech (medical devices), with a broad portfolio spanning oncology, immunology, neuroscience, and surgical/orthopedic device products.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Innovative Medicine segment revenue from key pharmaceutical franchises in oncology, immunology, and neuroscience
  • MedTech segment revenue from surgical, orthopedic, vision, and cardiovascular device products
  • Continued new product launches and pipeline advancement across both segments

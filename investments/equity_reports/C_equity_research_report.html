Business Description

Citigroup is a global diversified financial services company operating across Services, Markets, Banking, U.S. Personal Banking, and Wealth segments, with a distinctive global institutional banking network spanning both developed and emerging markets. In recent years, management has pursued a significant strategic simplification, including divesting or winding down a number of international consumer banking franchises to focus capital on higher-return institutional and wealth businesses.

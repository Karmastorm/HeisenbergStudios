Business Description

Cisco Systems, Inc. designs, manufactures, and sells networking hardware, software, and services, including switching and routing equipment, data center and AI networking infrastructure (Ethernet switches used in AI clusters), cybersecurity products, and collaboration tools (Webex). The 2024 acquisition of Splunk added observability and security analytics software capabilities, accelerating Cisco's mix shift toward higher-margin, recurring software and subscription revenue alongside its traditional hardware business.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • AI-driven data center networking equipment demand (high-speed Ethernet switches and related infrastructure) from hyperscale cloud customers
  • Security segment growth, including Splunk-driven observability and security analytics cross-sell into the existing networking customer base
  • Software/subscription mix shift across core networking and collaboration product lines, improving revenue recurrence

Key Expense Drivers

  • R&D investment in AI-optimized networking silicon and software, plus integration costs associated with the Splunk acquisition
  • Cost of hardware components and contract manufacturing for networking equipment production

The Splunk acquisition adds a differentiated observability/security analytics capability that deepens customer relationships.

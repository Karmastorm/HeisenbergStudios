Business Description

Albemarle Corporation is one of the world's largest producers of lithium compounds (lithium hydroxide, lithium carbonate) used primarily in electric-vehicle and grid-scale battery production, operating through Energy Storage, Specialties (bromine and other specialty chemicals), and Ktalco (catalysts) segments. The company's earnings are highly leveraged to global lithium prices, which experienced a severe multi-year collapse from 2022-2023 peaks amid a combination of new supply additions and slower-than-expected electric-vehicle demand growth, though recent quarters have shown signs of price stabilization.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Global lithium price, which is highly cyclical and sensitive to electric-vehicle demand growth, battery manufacturing capacity additions, and global lithium mining/processing supply growth
  • Lithium compound production volumes across the company's mining and processing operations
  • Bromine specialties and catalyst product sales, which provide a more stable, less cyclical revenue base than the lithium business

Key Expense Drivers

  • Mining, processing, and energy costs for lithium extraction and conversion, which have significant operating leverage given largely fixed production capacity
  • Capital expenditure on lithium production capacity expansion, though this has reportedly been scaled back amid the recent price downturn

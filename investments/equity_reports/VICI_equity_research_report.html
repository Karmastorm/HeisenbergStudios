Business Description

VICI Properties Inc. is a real estate investment trust that owns a portfolio of iconic gaming, hospitality, and entertainment properties, including numerous Las Vegas Strip casino resorts and regional gaming properties, which it leases to premier casino and entertainment operators under long-term, triple-net-lease agreements. Under this lease structure, tenants are responsible for substantially all property operating expenses (taxes, insurance, maintenance), producing VICI's characteristically very high operating and net margins relative to other real estate sub-sectors.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Contractual rent escalators embedded in VICI's long-term triple-net-lease agreements with major gaming and entertainment operators
  • Continued acquisition activity, expanding the portfolio's scale and tenant/geographic diversification beyond traditional Las Vegas Strip gaming exposure
  • Tenant rent coverage ratios, which reflect the underlying financial health of major tenants such as Caesars Entertainment and MGM Resorts

Key Expense Drivers

  • Interest expense on debt used to fund the company's ongoing acquisition activity, a key driver of net income given the REIT's otherwise very low property-level operating expense structure
  • General and administrative costs associated with managing the portfolio, a small expense category relative to the triple-net-lease revenue base

Business Description

Linde plc is the world's largest industrial gases company, producing and distributing atmospheric gases (oxygen, nitrogen, argon) and process gases (hydrogen, carbon dioxide, helium, electronic gases) to customers across healthcare, manufacturing, food and beverage, energy, chemicals, and semiconductor end markets. The company operates through a mix of on-site (pipeline-connected, long-term contracted) supply to large industrial customers, merchant (bulk liquid delivery) gas sales, and packaged/cylinder gas distribution, with an increasing focus on clean hydrogen and carbon capture infrastructure investments tied to the broader energy transition.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • On-site gas supply contracts with large industrial customers, typically long-term (15-20 year) and often including take-or-pay volume commitments providing highly visible, recurring revenue
  • Merchant and packaged gas sales tied to broader industrial production and healthcare demand
  • New project backlog conversion, particularly in clean hydrogen, carbon capture, and semiconductor/electronics gas supply, an increasingly important growth vector

Key Expense Drivers

  • Energy costs (electricity, natural gas) required for air separation and gas production, substantially passed through via contract pricing mechanisms
  • Capital expenditure on new on-site production facilities and pipeline infrastructure to serve contracted project backlog

Business Description

EOG Resources, Inc. is a leading independent oil and natural gas exploration and production company, with a core asset base concentrated in premier U.S. unconventional plays including the Delaware Basin, Eagle Ford, and Utica Shale, alongside newer exploration activity in the UAE and a recently expanded Austin Chalk position. The company is known industry-wide for its disciplined "premium" drilling-return framework and continuous well-cost reduction culture, which have delivered some of the lowest per-foot drilling costs in the industry across multiple basins. EOG maintains a low breakeven WTI price (below $50/barrel), supporting resilient free cash flow generation and substantial shareholder capital returns even in moderate commodity-price environments.

Business Description

Emerson Electric Co. is a global technology and software company providing automation solutions for process, hybrid, and discrete manufacturing industries, including oil and gas, chemical, power generation, life sciences, and food and beverage end markets. The company is organized into Final Control (valves, actuators, regulators), Measurement & Analytical (instrumentation and analytical measurement devices), and Control Systems & Software (including its majority-owned AspenTech industrial software business), and has pursued a portfolio transformation over recent years that has increased its exposure to higher-margin, recurring automation-software revenue.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Process automation capital spending by oil & gas, chemical, and power generation customers, tied to global industrial capital-expenditure cycles
  • Automation software and control-systems sales, an increasingly important higher-margin, more recurring revenue stream following the AspenTech combination
  • Final control and measurement instrumentation sales tied to plant maintenance, safety, and efficiency upgrade cycles

Key Expense Drivers

  • Raw material and component costs (metals, electronics), subject to commodity price and tariff volatility
  • R&D and integration investment supporting the automation-software platform and continued portfolio transformation

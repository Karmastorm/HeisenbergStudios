Valuation Summary

At $184.07, ACN trades at a compressed 15.2x trailing P/E — one of the cheapest absolute valuations in this report series' Information Technology sector — reflecting the large drawdown from its 52-week high and market concern about the pace of consulting-revenue growth amid client budget scrutiny and potential AI-driven disintermediation of traditional IT services work.

Business Description

Accenture plc is a global professional services company providing strategy, consulting, technology, and operations services, organized around industry-aligned groups spanning communications/media/technology, financial services, health/public services, products, and resources. The company helps large enterprises with digital transformation, cloud migration, and increasingly generative AI adoption and deployment, partnering with major cloud and AI platform providers (including a recently announced strategic partnership with Google Cloud) to deliver AI-enabled consulting and implementation services.

Key Revenue Drivers

  • Enterprise digital transformation and cloud migration consulting engagements across its industry-aligned client groups
  • Generative AI consulting, deployment, and managed services bookings, an increasingly emphasized growth vector
  • Outsourcing/managed services revenue, which provides a more recurring, stable revenue base relative to project-based consulting

Key Expense Drivers

  • Labor costs (consultant compensation and utilization rates), the dominant cost driver for a professional services firm
  • Investment in AI training/capability-building for its consulting workforce and technology partnerships

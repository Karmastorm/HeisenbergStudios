What it doesDesigns and manufactures chips across four lines — Client Computing (PC/laptop CPUs), Data Center & AI (server CPUs/AI silicon), Intel Foundry (contract manufacturing for outside customers), and Network & Edge — plus a majority stake in Mobileye. Most revenue still comes from selling its own branded chips; foundry service fees are a small but strategically important and growing slice.
What makes it differentOne of very few companies that both designs and manufactures leading-edge logic chips at scale — though its manufacturing lead over TSMC has eroded, and it's now racing to prove its 18A/14A nodes can win real outside customers.

GAAP NET LOSSES IN 2024 AND 2025
Net income fell from $19.9B (2021) to a $18.8B loss (2024) and a smaller $0.27B loss (2025); TTM ROE is -10.7%. Source: stockanalysis.com financials.
CREDIT RATING DOWNGRADED

MASSIVE DILUTION & NEW GOVERNMENT OWNERSHIP

Definitions — tap to expand

P/E ratio — What you pay per $1 of profit. Lower vs. history and sector = cheaper. Not meaningful when a company has no trailing profit.

P/B ratio — Price vs. what the company actually owns. Below 1 = buying at a discount to assets.

EV/EBITDA — Full business value check including debt. Lower = better value.

ROE — For every $100 shareholders invested, how much profit the company made. Above 15% = good.

ROIC / ROCE — How efficiently the whole business uses capital, including borrowed money. Above 15% = healthy.

Free Cash Flow — Cash left after all expenses and investments. Positive and growing = genuinely healthy business.

13F filing — A quarterly SEC disclosure large institutional investors (funds, banks, pensions) must file showing their US equity holdings — the US equivalent of tracking FII/DII activity in other markets.

CAGR — Compound Annual Growth Rate. The average yearly growth rate over a period; not meaningful when the underlying figure swings from positive to negative.
